Vermicompost

Vermicompost is an organic manure created by the breakdown of agricultural waste using earthworms. It contains beneficial microbes, essential nutrients, and organic matter that improve soil structure, fertility, and crop yield naturally.

Why India for Vermicompost?

India is one of the world’s leading producers of organic inputs like vermicompost. With abundant agricultural waste and a strong ecosystem of smallholder farmers, we produce high-quality vermicompost at scale using natural processes.

Indian vermicompost is widely recognized for its nutrient richness and microbial activity, making it ideal for sustainable farming practices globally. Our strategic geographic location and strong export infrastructure ensure timely delivery to markets in the Middle East, Africa, Europe, and the Americas.
